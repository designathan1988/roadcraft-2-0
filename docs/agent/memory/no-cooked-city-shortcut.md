The owner (2026-10-26) rejected "cook the 10 km city into shipped files" as a fix for the 232 s generation: the game is sold on Steam, the player creates their own city, saves it and reopens it. Any shortcut that makes only a shipped city fast is result make-up ("maquiar resultados"), i.e. fraud.

**Why:** the goal is to enter the game and really play a 10 km city the player made: generate it, save it, reopen it, edit it, with everything real.

**How to apply:** make the generator itself fast (parallel growth by districts across the job processes, real buildings and lots), make save and reopen of the player's own 10 km city fast, and verify every one of those steps on the 10 km city with numbers and photos. Never present a cooked file, a stand-in or a reduced city as the result. Related: [[no-fake-city-elements]], [[say-the-city-size-and-what-is-unmet]].
